# StageForge Graphics Pipeline, Measurement Probe & Preview Safety

StageForge renders exclusively on Three.js (`0.187.0`) and WebGL2 with zero additional rendering engines or required WebGPU paths.

## Output Pipeline & Controls (Device-Local)

Display quality controls live under **Display & safety** in the top bar (editor and Pitch Viewer) and are stored only on the current device under `localStorage["stageforge.render.v1"]`. They are never serialized into `.stageforge.show.json` files or `.stageforge.zip` bundles.

The post-processing chain preserves `NeutralToneMapping` (exposure `0.96`) and a single sRGB conversion via `OutputPass`:

| Antialiasing (`aa`) | Pass chain | Composer RT samples | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `auto` / `msaa` | `RenderPass` → optional `ScaledBloomPass` → `OutputPass` | 2 (clamped to `MAX_SAMPLES`) | Default behaviour; default canvas MSAA (`antialias: false`) is off because `OutputPass` draws a single fullscreen quad |
| `fxaa` | `RenderPass` → optional `ScaledBloomPass` → `OutputPass` → `FXAAPass` | 0 | Mutually exclusive with MSAA; `FXAAShader` runs after `OutputPass` on display-referred sRGB values (+1 draw call) |
| `none` | `RenderPass` → optional `ScaledBloomPass` → `OutputPass` | 0 | Lowest offscreen memory and fill rate |

Bloom controls (`bloom`):
- **`auto` / `full`**: full-resolution `UnrealBloomPass` (bright target at 1/2 drawing buffer). Automatically bypassed (`enabled = false`) whenever `bloomStrength <= 0.001` with zero bloom passes executed.
- **`half`**: `ScaledBloomPass` scaling bloom's internal bright and 5 mip targets by `0.5` (bright target at 1/4 drawing buffer; produces wider halos at lower fill cost — not a pixel-parity mode).
- **`off`**: removes and disposes `ScaledBloomPass`, releasing all 11 internal RGBA16F bloom render targets.

Sprint 23A promotes the device-local `lookOutput` foundation by default. It reuses that same pass and keeps
Neutral tone mapping and sRGB output order unchanged: high tier applies a restrained `0.72×` authored bloom
strength with radius `0.26` and threshold `0.74`; effective Reduced effects caps bloom to half resolution with
`0.36×`, radius `0.18`, and threshold `0.80`. True colour removes bloom while retaining exposure, Neutral tone
mapping, sRGB output, and the video correctness decode. No mode adds a pass, draw, texture, geometry, or
application-owned GPU allocation. See [`docs/looks.md`](looks.md) for the approved reference frames and budgets.

## Scenery LOD, Seeded Particles & Falling CO2

- **Scenery LOD (`src/engine/scenicProps.ts`)**: High tier keeps the full original inflatable and star tessellation (`arch` 16,320 tube tris, `tentacle` 19,968 decorative tris, `star` 12,600 tris). Low tier reduces tube subdivisions to `20` sides (preserving 5-rib symmetry) × `6` rows/panel, `6 × 10` suction cups, and `18 × 60` star sectors with zero high-frequency wrinkle (~69–83% triangle reduction). GLB export always reconstructs canonical high-detail scenery regardless of preview tier.
- **Seeded Fixed-Step Particles (`src/engine/ParticleSystem.ts`)**: Smoke, cryo, and haze each draw from independent `mulberry32` streams with a fixed `1/60 s` simulation step (capped at 6 substeps per frame so tab resumes never emit bursts) and sub-frame position interpolation. Capacities are tiered (`high`: 260 smoke / 420 cryo / 36 haze; `low`: 120 / 180 / 18).
- **Falling CO2 (`fallingCo2`, default off)**: A **stylised dense-vapour approximation** (not a fluid simulation) using a two-component exponential jet + cold-sink integrator that arcs up ~2.1–3.1 m, stalls around 0.6 s, sinks back toward the stage deck, and spreads outward along the floor clamp.
- **Video sRGB Decode (`lookOutput` / internal `videoSrgbDecode` sub-gate, default on)**: controlled SMPTE intermediate-grey and 75% colour-bar evidence verifies that the custom `LED_WALL_FRAG` path needs an explicit sRGB EOTF for `VideoTexture`. The promoted foundation applies it only to video textures on main and authored secondary LED walls; ordinary sRGB images keep Three.js's existing decode and are not decoded twice. Old `stageforge.render.v1` objects migrate from the previous experimental default-off value when they lack the new `lookOutput` key.
- **Prism Floor Projections (`prismPools`, default off, Sprint 16)**: Extends `BeamPools` (`src/engine/beamPools.ts`, `src/engine/prismPattern.ts`) with each beam fixture's orthonormalized transverse frame and rotating 8-shaft prism phase (`uTime * 0.48 + uSeed`), evaluating a derivative-filtered anisotropic 2D Gaussian pattern (`Σ = σ² I + (Jx Jxᵀ + Jy Jyᵀ) / 12`) in receiver coordinates while preserving incidence, attenuation, and platform blockers in **0 added draw calls**. When `prismPools` is `false` (default), zero optional attributes, defines, or uniforms are allocated. When enabled, optional `prismU`/`prismV` streams (`32 B/instance`) are admitted under a `100 KiB` (`< 0.1 MiB`) application-buffer cap (driver-internal alignment overhead is outside this application calculation), and low tier suppresses prism evaluation with hysteresis if receiver-qualified overlapping prism pools exceed budget (`> 36` on one receiver or `> 68` total, resuming at `≤ 28` and `≤ 52`). Exact aerial-to-floor shaft alignment holds for orthogonal StageEngine transforms (hung and floor heads, pan/tilt, uniform zoom); synthetic sheared matrices fall back to a Gram–Schmidt orthonormalized receiver basis.

  Robe documents a six-facet linear prism for the MegaPointe. Explicitly selected MegaPointe beam and floor-beam fixtures preview that topology under the existing Gobo prism control. StageForge spaces the six centres at ±0.142, ±0.426 and ±0.71 of the beam radius at full reach, with the same rotation and reach ramps as the generic circular prism. The receiver normalizes the filtered spots within the same unit disk. Batched aerial beams and prism-enabled receivers carry the topology in the sign of their existing mix value. The magnitude remains the governed blend. [Robe MegaPointe user manual](https://www.robe.cz/api/support-files/download/downloads/user_manuals/User_manual_Robin_MegaPointe.pdf)
- **LED Wall Viewing-Angle Response (`ledAngularResponse`, default off, Sprint 17)**: Specializes `LED_WALL_VERT` and `LED_WALL_FRAG` (`src/engine/shaders.ts`, `src/engine/ledWallResponse.ts`) behind `#ifdef LED_ANGULAR_RESPONSE` across all primary, curved (`THREE.BackSide` via Three.js `FLIP_SIDED` vertex-normal negation + `gl_FrontFacing` double-sided guard), Side IMAG wing, and authored secondary LED screens in **0 added draw calls** and **0 B added GPU buffers/textures**. When `ledAngularResponse` is `false` (default), zero extra varyings, uniforms, or ALU instructions exist in the compiled program. When enabled, per-eye view-space normal and view vectors (`normalMatrix * normal`, `-(modelViewMatrix * position).xyz` — stereo-ready with zero CPU camera caching) drive a smooth rational emissivity curve `0.30 + 0.70 × (1.22 c² / (c² + 0.22))` (`c = clamp(dot(N, V), 0, 1)`), preserving full frontal readability (`≥ 0.955` within `30°`), bounded grazing falloff (`0.30` floor at `90°`), exact `uBrightness = 0` black output, and existing `fwidth(diode)` / `uPixelSnap` / `uCabinetGrid` moiré suppression. Stage spill lighting continues to use the authored primary/accent show palette (never synchronous video pixel readback).
- **Deck Sheen Response (`deckSheen`, default off, Sprint 18)**: Extends the seeded Marley stage-deck generator (`src/engine/hardwareMaterials.ts`, `src/engine/deckSheen.ts`) so panel seams and scuff marks interrupt specular highlights from the existing stage spill lights and PMREM environment on `MeshStandardMaterial` (`mats.stageDeck`) in **0 added draw calls** (no planar reflections or SSR). When `deckSheen` is `false` (default), zero optional arrays, canvases, or textures are allocated (`0 B`). When enabled, `DeckSheenController` replays `STAGE_DECK_SEED` (`0x71b3e905`) and 2×2 box-downsamples into linear (`THREE.NoColorSpace`) `RepeatWrapping` (`6 × 3`) mipmapped maps: **low tier** allocates one `128×128` roughness map (`87,380 B` / `0.0833 MiB ≤ 0.10 MiB` application payload including mipmaps, `normalMap = null`), and **high tier** allocates shared `256×256` roughness and weak tangent-space normal maps (`699,048 B` / `0.6667 MiB ≤ 0.70 MiB` application payload including mipmaps; GPU driver alignment overhead is outside this application calculation). Disabling `deckSheen` detaches and disposes the optional maps immediately, and `stageSnapshot` (`src/engine/stageGlb.ts`) strips `deckSheen` overrides so GLB exports are unaffected by local `deckSheen` state. (On legacy non-platform rigs, `BoxGeometry` continues to share `stageDeck` across all box faces; authored platform rigs apply `stageDeck` only to horizontal `Deck surface` tops.)
- **Wet deck composition (`lookWetDeck`, default on, Sprint 23C)**: On high tier, replaces the source scene for the single construction-time PMREM target with five narrow near-neutral strips and a darker neutral background. It adds no target, draw, pass, texture, geometry, buffer, or application GPU byte. With Sprint 18's optional maps off, the shared deck material derives conservative spatial roughness from the already-sampled seeded Marley albedo; explicit `deckSheen` maps remain authoritative. The PMREM represents distant lighting only, never position-correct fixtures, LED walls, or media. True colour retains the neutral PBR response because C adds no tint; Reduced effects and reduced motion retain it because it is static. Low tier uses the legacy PMREM/material path after the high-tier shader exceeded the phone-like software-renderer proxy budget. See `docs/looks.md` for fixed references, exact timings, lifecycle evidence, and limitations.
- **Crowd Show-Light Response (`crowdLighting`, default off, Sprint 19)**: Adds restrained directional wrap and light-side/forward-scatter-gated rim terms (`src/engine/crowdLighting.ts`) to the instanced crowd's `MeshStandardMaterial` while preserving the existing instanced bodies, morph poses, floor contact patches, and shared-`instanceMatrix` phone screens in **0 added draw calls** and **0 B added GPU buffers or textures** (an **artistic lighting approximation**, not per-person beam tracing, individual Three.js lights, or skinned/GPU-rewritten animation). When `crowdLighting` is `false` (default), the venue's base `MeshStandardMaterial` is untouched and zero aggregate crowd-light work runs. When enabled, all inputs come strictly from preview-flash-governed values (`0` contribution under blackout or zero master): **low tier** compiles `CROWD_SHOW_LIGHTS = 1` (single aggregate show-light direction and colour), and **high tier** compiles `CROWD_SHOW_LIGHTS = 2` (smoothly cross-faded house-left and house-right show-light directions and colours with energy normalized across slots). Directions transform into view space via Three.js's per-eye `viewMatrix` (stereo-safe for WebXR), an anatomical height mask keeps lower legs grounded while lifting shoulders/heads/raised arms, and a hue-preserving headroom clamp guarantees that **crowd show-light additions cannot push any RGB channel above `0.56` (below the `0.66` bloom threshold), while pre-existing direct/indirect lighting already above `0.56` is left untouched**.
- **Locally Beam-Lit Smoke & CO2 Puffs (`litPuffs`, default off, Sprint 20)**: Extends `AtmosphericParticles` (`src/engine/ParticleSystem.ts`, `src/engine/litPuffs.ts`) with per-instance illumination, intra-batch back-to-front depth sorting, and stereo-safe near-camera eye-depth fading while keeping `FogExp2`, analytic beam cones, and the `haze` batch in **0 added draw calls** (an **experimental bounded approximation**, not fluid simulation, fan response, or volumetric light-path shadow occlusion). When `litPuffs` is `false` (default), zero optional CPU state arrays or GPU buffers are allocated (`0 B`) and `smoke`/`cryo` preserve their base shaders, slot order, and `renderOrder = 0`. When enabled:
  - **Capped CPU candidate selection & per-puff top-K evaluation**: Selects a stable candidate set of active preview-flash-governed moving-head beams once per frame (`8` on **low tier**, `16` on **high tier**) using max proximity across separate smoke-cloud, cryo-cloud, and active-emitter bounds plus `1.18×` retention hysteresis and deterministic ascending `fixture.index` tie-breaking, then evaluates **at most `2` (`low`) or `4` (`high`) beam contributions per puff** on the CPU (`0` shader loops, `0` dynamic uniform-array indexing, WebGL1 & WebGL2 compatible).
  - **Single dominant localization lobe + broad scatter**: Optional `broadLight` (`vec3`), `lobeLight` (`vec3`), and world-space `lobeOffset` (`vec4`, `40 B/instance`: `12,000 B` / `0.0114 MiB` on `low` tier, `27,200 B` / `0.0259 MiB` on `high` tier — both `< 0.10 MiB` application buffer ceiling; driver alignment overhead is outside this application calculation) localize the strongest narrow beam shaft onto the intersected side of the rotated billboard via `mat2(c, -s, s, c) * (modelViewMatrix * vec4(offset, 0.0)).xy` while summing secondary crossing beams into broad cloud scatter. A `45 ms` per-puff filter softens candidate/rank handovers during motion while blackout zeroes local puff light immediately.
  - **Ordering, near-camera fade & radiance bounds**: Upload indices sort back-to-front by planar view depth (`spawnSeq` tie-breaker so pool slot compaction and cryo eviction never flip surviving puffs, and physics/RNG state stays untouched), while `smoke` vs `cryo` batch `renderOrder` (`1` vs `2`) follows opacity-weighted mean camera depth with a `0.30 m` deadband (batch-level ordering cannot resolve per-particle cross-batch intersections). Unlit background smoke/cryo ambient wash scales down (`0.38×` smoke / `0.55×` cryo above the `0.025`/`0.018` dark floor) so puffs outside beams stay moody rather than uniformly lit, a per-eye `[0.35 m, 2.05 m]` camera-depth fade softens near-camera overdraw and near-plane clipping (it cannot cast a truss shadow along a beam), and a hue-preserving soft knee (`≤ 0.68`, after `1.15` smoke and `1.28` cryo scatter gains) bounds **added local beam radiance** (base cloud tint and overlapping additive beams can still contribute above `0.68`).
- **ILDA Scan-Energy Ribbons (`ildaScanEnergy`, default off, Sprint 21)**: Replaces each active ILDA projector's legacy `LineSegments` draw with one camera-facing triangle ribbon draw using a bright core and a haze-scaled soft halo (`src/engine/ildaScanEnergy.ts`); it is a bounded artistic scan-energy appearance, **not a physical galvo simulation**. Consecutive equal-position dwell points compact into one rendered ray with a clamped energy weight, sharp within-contour corners receive a bounded lift, blanking always breaks adjacency, and original `sourcePointCount` values remain unchanged for kpps/refresh timing. Per-eye `modelViewMatrix` expansion uses a deterministic near-parallel fallback, drops degenerate segments, fades within `0.30–1.0 m`, and suppresses segments crossing the near plane. Allocation is divided across every live projector: at most **4,000 added triangles / 480,000 B on low tier** and **16,000 triangles / 1,920,000 B on high tier** (below the 0.5/2 MiB ceilings). If a frame/mode cannot fit its projector allotment, the whole projector uses the exact legacy line fallback for that frame; output is never truncated and exactly one path is visible, preserving draw count. Disabling the flag immediately disposes all optional geometry/materials. Physical DMX, pre-flight, ILDA ingestion, show files, GLB export, and the preview flash governor are unchanged.

## Preview Flash Limiter & Scoped Safety Claims

By default, StageForge runs a preview-only flash governor (`src/engine/previewFlash.ts`, backed by `src/engine/flashGovernor.ts`) alongside the physical DMX flash governors (`src/engine/audioModulation.ts`):
- Enforces **≤ 3 general and ≤ 3 saturated-red flashes in any rolling 1,000 ms window** across manual/audio beam, wash, and strobe-bar strobing, multi-frame 60/120 Hz sawtooth ramps, procedural laser shader blanking (single shared phase across all projectors), laser `enabled` / momentary `b.laser` / `intensity` / custom-colour flips, ILDA low-kpps refresh shimmer (steady time-average intensity; scan-rate diagnostics stay unchanged), LED wall `strobe_bars` / audio-band / beat flashes, rapid blinder taps, blackout recovery, rapid `goboPrism` centre-vs-ring spatial redistribution toggles, and rapid cue, group-colour, and `colorMix` flips (holding rapid rises and opposing colour/red swaps across the rolling 1 s reversal window while keeping emergency blackout and downward dimming immediate).
- **Physical DMX isolation**: The **Full-rate strobe preview** opt-in is session-only (never persisted), requires an explicit confirmation dialog focused on Cancel, displays an always-visible `FULL-RATE STROBE` badge, is disabled (and cleared if previously enabled) under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and in Pitch Viewer, and **never alters `dmxUniverse1/2` or `physicalDmxU1/U2`**.
- **Scope and limitations**: Automated tests verify bounded behaviour on StageForge's rendered output across the tested strobe, ramp, laser, ILDA, LED-wall, blinder, blackout, and cue/colour-change scenarios. **These checks show bounded behaviour on tested paths, not universal photosensitivity safety.** Uploaded user video/media, arbitrary imported ILDA frame-to-frame geometry or vertex-colour sequences, and rapid manual camera sweeps are outside parameter-level preview flash governing unless verified via frame-level readback (`analyzeRollingFlashRate`).

## Graphics Measurement Probe (`?probe`)

Loading with `?probe` exposes measurement hooks and `?probe`-only test harness controls (never exposed to normal sessions or the AI bridge):
- `renderStats()`: preserves all existing whole-frame counters (`calls`, `triangles`, `geometries`, `textures`, `programs`, `objects`, `meshes`, `visibleMeshes`, `autoUpdating`, `batching`) with `visibleMeshes` counting ancestor-visible meshes whose layers intersect `camera.layers`.
- `graphicsProbe()`: reports `drawingBufferSize`, `cssSize`, DPR, `tier`, `backend`, `contextAntialias`, `composerSamplesEffective`, `activePasses`, bloom allocation/activity, particle counts/caps, `prismPools` diagnostics, `ledWalls` angular-specialization diagnostics, `deckSheen` material-response diagnostics, the one wet-deck environment target's composition/identity/descriptor/build count (including deterministic replacement after real context restore), `crowd` show-light response & phone-attachment diagnostics, ILDA line/ribbon segments, fallback counts, allocated triangle/byte ceilings, `submittedMeshes` (draw-submitted `Mesh` objects, counting an `InstancedMesh` as one submitted mesh), and `renderTargetBytesEstimate`.
- `measureFrames({ warmupFrames, sampleFrames, gpu })`: collects zero-allocation preallocated CPU sub-phase timings (`simMs`, `crowdMs`, `particlesMs`, `batchSyncMs` subtracted from `renderSubmitMs` so nothing is double-counted), upload byte estimates, and optional asynchronous non-blocking WebGL2 `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` timings (started only after warm-up frames complete, rejecting disjoint epochs, never calling `gl.finish()`).
- `?probe`-only test controls (`setRenderSettings` in-memory without writing `localStorage`, `setFullRateStrobePreview`, `setCameraPose`, `resetParticles`, `stepAndReadLuminance`, `patchShowState`, `setProbeBump`, `loadIldaClip`) drive automated regression benchmarks without altering persisted device preferences.

Keep SwiftShader correctness runs, 4× CPU-throttled desktop runs, laptop hardware runs, and real phone measurements separate; CPU throttling in headless Chrome does not emulate a phone GPU.
